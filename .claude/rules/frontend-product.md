# Frontend Product Guidance

- Frontend should render backend truth-state rather than invent product logic in the client.
- Preserve the guided coach flow; do not turn the UI into a generic assistant shell.
- Show scope, mission, evidence, and adaptation signals clearly when the backend provides them.
- Keep cold-start states honest; do not fake evidence or readiness when the backend has none.
