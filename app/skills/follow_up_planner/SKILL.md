---
id: follow_up_planner
title: Follow-Up Planner
description: Convert evidence from a session into the next concrete learning action.
kind: career_loop_skill
inputs:
  - latest_evidence
  - goal_brief
  - proficiency_profile
outputs:
  - next_mission
  - improvement_focus
---
Plan the most useful next learning action from the latest evidence.

Priority:
- keep the mission concrete
- tie it to the learner's job goal
- choose one main blocker
- explain why this mission matters now

Avoid generic advice like "practice more English".
