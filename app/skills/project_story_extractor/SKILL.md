  - user_project_notes
  - target_role
outputs:
  - project_story_pack
---
Help the coach turn raw project notes into 2-3 clear project walkthrough stories.

Each story should be useful for:
- interview answers
- recruiter conversations
- explaining impact and trade-offs

Prioritize:
- the problem
- the user's contribution
- technical choices
- metrics or impact
- clear spoken-English phrasing
