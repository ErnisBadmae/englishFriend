title: Vacancy Analysis
description: Turn a target vacancy into a sharper career-English goal and practice focus.
kind: career_loop_skill
inputs:
  - vacancy_text
  - goal_brief
outputs:
  - enriched_goal_brief
  - vocabulary_focus
  - interview_focus
---
Analyze a target vacancy and extract only the signals that matter for the career-English coach.

Output should help the coach answer:
- what role the learner is targeting
- what speaking contexts matter most
- what technical and behavioral vocabulary to train
- what interview and project explanation angles are likely to matter

Do not act like a general job-search assistant.
Stay focused on language, communication, and job-readiness.
